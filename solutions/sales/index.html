How it works

You set the target. The agents keep working until it's hit.

  1. Set the goal

    Pick a premade agent or describe the outcome, then agree the metric and target with us.

  2. Run

    Agents work through your tools on a schedule, with your approval on anything that goes out.

  3. Measure

    Every run records what it did and what it moved, so you see progress, not activity.

  4. Auto-improve

    It fixes what broke, drops what didn't work and tries the next idea.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will it send emails without me?

Not unless you allow it. By default every outbound email waits for your approval, and you can raise the limit once you trust it.

Does it work with our CRM?

Yes, through MCP or the CRM's website in the agent's own browser. HubSpot, Notion and Airtable are common starting points.

How is this different from a sales sequencer?

A sequencer sends what you wrote on a schedule. AgentWorks owns the goal: it measures bookings, drops what doesn't work and tries what might.
