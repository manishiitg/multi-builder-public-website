The problem

Each person’s AI works alone. The work between people still moves by hand.

  • Every chat starts from zero

    Each person explains the customer, the product and the task again in their own chat.

  • People carry the work

    Message, ticket, someone’s AI chat, stand-up question. Every step waits for someone to notice.

  • Nobody sees where it stands

    Who has it, which agent did what, what’s stuck? The answer is spread across private chats.

How it works

Ask. Hand off. Keep it running.

  1. Ask any agentAnyone asks the team’s agents for data, status or a draft, from the app, Slack, WhatsApp or their own AI tool. The agents work in your real tools.
  2. Hand work offAgents pass work to each other and to people, across teams, laptops and the server, through AgentBridge. A person is asked only when the decision is theirs.
  3. Keep it runningAgents stay online and do routine work on a schedule: reports, checks and follow-ups, even when everyone is offline.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? Book 30 minutes with us.

Do we have to change our tools?

No. Jira, Notion, Google Docs, Sheets, Slack and the rest stay where they are. AgentWorks connects your team's agents to them, so agents can open the ticket, fill the sheet or post the summary, and adds the part that was missing: who does it next and where it stands.

Is AgentWorks only for engineering teams?

No. Sales, support, finance, marketing, operations and product teams use it the same way: ask an agent, hand work off, and let routine jobs run. Developers can bring their coding agents too.

Does my team need to know how to code?

No. People ask agents in plain words, from the app, Slack or WhatsApp. Premade agents cover common jobs, and we set up new ones with you.

Which AI plans can we use?

The ones you already pay for: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor and others, through each vendor's own agent. Each person signs in with their own plan, or the team uses a shared key.

Where does it run?

On your own server, or on a Mac. Your work, files and chats stay in your environment; AI requests go only to the providers you connect. The source code is public.

Can an agent send something without approval?

Not unless you allow it. Emails, messages, payments and posts wait for a person by default. You can raise the limits per agent once you trust it.
