Premade agents

Premade agents, ready to start today. 79 of them.

Agents for sales, support, finance, marketing, operations, product and engineering. Your team asks them in the app, Slack, WhatsApp or its own AI, hands them work, and lets their routine jobs run. We adapt them to your tools and rules during setup. Each ships with its setup steps, the tools it needs and guardrails that ask before anything reaches a customer. The source is public: read any of them, and modify them locally for development and testing.
