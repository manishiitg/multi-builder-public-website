How it works

Everyone’s AI agents, working as one team. On top of the tools you already use.

Everyone on your team has AI, but each person’s AI works alone and the work between people still moves by hand. AgentWorks connects them: anyone hands work to an agent, agents pass it on, and everyone sees where it stands.

Book a call Try the demo

Three things

Ask. Hand off. Keep it running.

  1. AskAnyone asks the team’s agents for data, status or a draft, from the app, Slack, WhatsApp or their own AI tool. The agents work in the company’s real tools, so nobody waits for the one person who knows.
  2. Hand offAny person or agent hands work to another agent and gets the result back, across teams, laptops and the server. A person is pulled in only when the decision is theirs.
  3. Keep it runningAgents stay online and run routine work on a schedule: the daily report, the follow-ups, the checks. It is done before anyone asks.

One piece of work, across the team

What it looks like. Illustrative example.

QA finds a broken checkout button the day before a release.

  1. QA hands it offThe tester tells their agent what broke. It opens the Jira ticket with the steps and a screenshot.
  2. The developer’s agent picks it upIt lands as a task with the developer’s agent, details attached, and the fix starts.
  3. A person decidesThe developer reviews the change. Nothing ships without them.
  4. Everyone sees itThe PM asks “is checkout fixed?” and gets the answer from an agent, without pinging anyone.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do we have to replace Jira, Notion or Slack?

No. Your tools stay where they are. Agents work inside them through connections set up once in the Team kit (open the ticket, fill the sheet, post the summary), and AgentWorks adds the missing part: who does it next and where it stands.

Is it only for engineering teams?

No. Sales, support, finance, marketing, operations, product and engineering use it the same way. Developers keep their worktrees, pull requests, editor and terminal; everyone else works in chat.

Which AI does it use?

The plans your people already pay for: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor and others, through each vendor's own agent. People keep the AI tool they like, side by side on the same team, with no new per-seat AI bill.

Where does it run?

On your own server, or on a Mac. Your work, files and chats stay in your environment; AI requests go only to the providers you connect. The source is public, so your security team can read it.

Can an agent send something on its own?

Not unless you allow it. Emails, messages, payments and posts wait for a person by default.
