What it does

  • Run a process toward a number

    Give agents a process and a target, like collecting overdue invoices or replying to every new lead within ten minutes. They run it on schedule, every run is measured, and the plan is improved from what the numbers show.

Control

Built for how companies buy software. Not a tool someone installed on a laptop.

  • Your cloud

    Deployed in your VPC or private cloud, customized to your processes, tools and approval rules.

  • Single sign-on

    SAML or OIDC sign-in, with access by team and person. Being in the company is not access; sharing is.

  • Every run recorded

    What was asked, what each agent did, what it cost and who approved it, visible to the people the work is shared with.

  • People approve what matters

    Outgoing messages, payments and changes wait for a named person unless you allow them.

  • Your AI plans

    Runs on the Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Cursor plans you already pay for. No new model contract.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do we have to buy each product separately?

No. AgentWorks is one platform. We start with one process your team wants handed to agents, set up the parts it needs, and add more as you go.

Where does it run?

In your own cloud or private infrastructure, with single sign-on. Your data and credentials stay with you.

Which AI models does it use?

The Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Cursor plans your company already pays for, through each vendor's own agent. You can change models without rebuilding the work.

How do people stay in control?

Anything that goes out, such as emails, messages or payments, waits for a person's approval unless you allow it. Every run is recorded with what it did, what it cost and who approved it.
