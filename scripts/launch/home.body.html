Goals

Other agents finish the task. Goals measures the number.

Chat bots and assistants stop when the job is done. A Goal keeps running inside your process, measures what every run changed, and shows what is moving the metric, so your team can decide what to do next.

  1. Set the goal

    Say what you want in plain words, pick the number that proves it, and set the target.

  2. Run

    Agents plan the steps, connect your tools and run on schedule, with your approval on anything that goes out.

  3. Measure

    Every run records what happened and moves the metric. You see progress, not activity.

  4. Improve

    Goals compares the result with the target, fixes what broke and changes the plan, then runs again.
