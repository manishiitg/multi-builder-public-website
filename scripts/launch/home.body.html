The problem

Each person’s AI works alone. The work between people still moves by hand.

  • Every chat starts from zero

    Each person explains the customer, the product and the task again in their own chat.

  • People carry the work

    Message, ticket, someone’s AI chat, stand-up question. Every step waits for someone to notice.

  • Nobody sees where it stands

    Who has it, which agent did what, what’s stuck? The answer is spread across private chats.

How it works

Ask. Hand off. Keep it running.

  1. Ask any agentAnyone asks the team’s agents for data, status or a draft, from the app, Slack, WhatsApp or their own AI tool. The agents work in your real tools.
  2. Hand work offAgents pass work to each other and to people, across teams, laptops and the server, through AgentBridge. A person is asked only when the decision is theirs.
  3. Keep it runningAgents stay online and do routine work on a schedule: reports, checks and follow-ups, even when everyone is offline.
