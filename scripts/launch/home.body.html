For the people who build agents

On your laptop, it helps you. Online, it works for your whole team.

Building an agent is the easy part now. Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or Claude will get you a working automation in an afternoon. What's missing is everything after.

On your laptop

  • Runs when your laptop is on and you remember to start it
  • Only you can use it
  • Your keys and logins sit on your machine
  • Nobody sees what it did, or what it cost
  • Five automations, five people, nothing shared
  • When you move on, it leaves with you

On AgentWorks

  • Runs on a schedule, on a trigger, or when someone asks
  • Your team uses it from Slack, WhatsApp, a dashboard or their own AI
  • Secrets stay in Vault, and IT decides what each agent can touch
  • Every run is recorded: the steps, the result, the cost and who asked
  • Goals, Crews and Flows run side by side and share one Brain
  • It belongs to the team, and it keeps improving
  1. Build it where you already workKeep using Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or Claude. Build on AgentWorks from there over MCP, or describe it to the Builder.
  2. Share it onceGive it an Ask box, a few buttons and a dashboard. Your team reaches it from Slack, WhatsApp, the app or their own AI.
  3. Run it, see it, improve itSchedules and triggers run it. Every run is recorded. Autopilot shows what to fix, and people approve what matters.

From your own terminal: everything above is reachable from the Claude Code, Codex or Cursor you already use, over MCP and the agentworks command line.
