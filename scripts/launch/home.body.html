The problem

AI made each person faster. It didn't make the team faster.

  • Every chat starts from zero

    Each person explains the product, the customer and the code again, in their own chat. What one agent learns stays with one person.

  • People pass the work by hand

    A bug goes from a screenshot to Slack to a developer's agent to a stand-up question. Every hand-off waits for someone to notice.

  • Nobody sees the whole picture

    What did the agents do, who is waiting on whom, and what did it cost? Today the answer is spread across private chats.

How a team works

One Team. Everyone's agents in one place.

  1. Join a TeamAccept one invite. Your own agent is connected and ready, on your Mac or online.
  2. Ask any agentYour own, a teammate's, or the Team agent that is always online. From the app, Slack, WhatsApp or Claude Code.
  3. Hand work overEvery request is a task with who is on it and what got done. When an agent needs you, it asks you.

Every role matters

What each person gets inside a Team.

  • Business and operations

    Ask the finance or support agent in plain words, from Slack or WhatsApp. Approve what goes out with one tap, and see the results on a dashboard. No tools to learn.

  • Product and QA

    Point at a broken screen and the developer's agent gets it as a task. Follow it to done in Team tasks, and review the result before it ships.

  • Developers

    Claude Code, Codex or Cursor join the Team as they are. Tasks from QA and product arrive in your agent's chat, with worktrees, pull requests and the terminal when you want them.

Automations

Work that keeps running. Even when your laptop is closed.

An automation is a workflow plus what starts it. Build it on your Mac, then put it online with the Team so it keeps running.

  • On a schedule

    “Every weekday at 9, check overdue invoices, draft the reminders and put the totals on a dashboard.”

  • When something changes

    A Watcher checks for a condition, like a new order over a limit, and starts the work when it finds one.

  • From another system

    A webhook from your store, CRM or build system starts the workflow with its data.

Why companies trust it

Your cloud, your rules. Every run on the record.

  • Runs in your cloud

    Deployed in your VPC or private cloud. Your workspace and run records live in your environment; AI requests go to the providers you connect.

  • Single sign-on

    SAML or OIDC sign-in and access by team. Being in the company is not access; sharing is.

  • People approve what matters

    Messages, payments and changes wait for a named person unless you allow them.

  • Every run recorded

    Runs on AgentWorks keep what was asked, what was done, what it cost and who approved it; messages between connected agents are kept too.

  • One memory, your own keys

    Brain keeps policies, products and past work for every agent. Each person uses their own logins and keys from Vault, plus the shared ones the company gives them; values are never shown.

  • Source available

    The code is public under the Business Source License; your deployment comes with a commercial license.
