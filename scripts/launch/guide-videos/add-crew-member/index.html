Next: talk to your crew in Slack
Crew members are AI teammates that are always on.
To add one, switch to Crew at the top left.
Then press plus, next to the crew member's name.
Start from a template, or from blank.
Give them an icon, a name, and one line about what they're for.
Choose Create crew member.
A new crew member asks for a role and a purpose.
Choose Set them up, and answer a few quick questions in chat.
Templates come with their own setup steps, so your new teammate knows exactly what to ask you.
