Next: set up your first goal
To talk to your crew on WhatsApp, open Integrations, then WhatsApp, and choose Open.
You connect WhatsApp once, for your whole account.
On your phone, open Linked devices, and choose Link a device.
Scan the code, and give the number a name, so your crew knows who's writing.
Now just message them. Ask a question, approve a reply, or hand off a job, from wherever you are.
