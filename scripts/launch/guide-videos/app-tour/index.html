Here's a quick tour. At the top left, you switch between AgentWorks, for automations that work toward a goal, and Crew, for your always-on AI teammates.
Next to it is the automation you're working in.
Switch automations here, or press plus to create a new one.
On the right are Providers, for your AI plans.
Activity, for every run and every decision waiting for you.
And Schedules, for what runs when.
Each automation has two halves.
On the left, you talk to the builder in plain words.
On the right is its workspace, starting with the dashboard.
The toolbar switches the workspace.
Auto-improve, decisions, activity, the plan, its browser and its automations.
Setup holds its identity, integrations and playbooks.
