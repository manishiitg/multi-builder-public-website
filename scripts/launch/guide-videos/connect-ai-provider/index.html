Next: create your first automation
Every agent in AgentWorks runs on an AI plan you already pay for.
So the first step is to connect one.
Open Providers from the top bar.
You'll see Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Pi and Muse, each with its status.
Pick one. Here, Claude Code is already connected, and ready for workflows.
Want to use your own subscription instead of the shared account?
Choose Add account, and paste the token from your own login.
You can check your usage at any time, so you always know how much of your plan is left.
Now open an automation, and go to Setup, then Identity, then Models.
Choose Use next to your provider.
That's it. Your agents are ready to work.
