How we work

We set it up with you. Your team runs it.

In four weeks we set AgentWorks up with one of your teams, on your servers: one piece of work they pass around by hand, the tools it touches, and the agents that do it. Then your team runs it, and you add the next team when it works.

Four weeks

One team first. Then the next.

  1. Step 1 · 30 minutes

    Pick the work on a call

    Which team, which piece of work moves between people by hand, which tools it touches and who decides what. We tell you honestly if it’s a fit.

  2. Step 2 · Four weeks

    We set it up with your team

    We install AgentWorks on your servers, connect your tools in the Team kit, add your company knowledge to Brain, and build the agents with the people who do the work.

  3. Step 3 · After

    Your team runs it

    The team asks its agents, hands work off and lets routine jobs run. When it works, the next team joins on the same setup.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Book 30 minutes with us.

What does the four-week setup include?

We pick one team and one piece of work they pass around by hand. We install AgentWorks on your servers, connect the tools that work touches, set up the Team kit, and build the agents with your people. At the end your team runs it.

How is it priced?

A fixed setup with a clear scope, then the product licence. We agree both on a call once we know the team and the work.

Do we have to change our tools?

No. Jira, Notion, Google Docs and Sheets, Slack and the rest stay. Agents work inside them.

Do we need new AI subscriptions?

No. Each person uses the AI plan they already pay for (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor and others), or the team uses a shared key. We never mark up model costs.

Is the source code available?

Yes. The source is public under the Business Source License 1.1. Using it in the operation of your business needs a commercial licence from us, which comes with the setup. Each version converts to the Apache License 2.0 after three years.

What happens after the first team?

Add the next team when the first one works. Its agents use the same Team kit, Brain and tool connections, so each team starts faster.
