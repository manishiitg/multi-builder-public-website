# Runloop Marketing Positioning Notes

These notes capture the keyword research and copy reasoning behind the homepage direction. Keep this file updated when the site positioning changes.

## Research Snapshot

Google Trends checks were run for the United States over the past 12 months.

Core positioning terms:

- `AI automation` and `AI agents` showed much stronger demand than `workflow automation`.
- `AI automation platform` performed well as a commercial category phrase.
- `AI agents for business` showed strong intent for business buyers.
- `business process automation` remains a useful secondary phrase, especially for buyers who do not yet search for agent-specific terms.
- `workflow automation` should be used as supporting language, not the primary homepage frame.

Use-case terms:

- `support automation` and `AI customer support` are stronger than generic support workflow language.
- `social media automation` is stronger than narrower channel-only positioning.
- `QA automation` and `automation testing` are clearer than QA workflow.
- `cloud cost optimization` and `cloud cost optimization tools` are clearer for the cloud costs page.
- `AI lead generation` has more demand than `lead enrichment automation`, though lead enrichment still describes the actual product use case.

## Positioning Decision

The homepage should lead with:

- Primary category: `AI automation platform`
- Primary buyer phrase: `AI agents for business`
- Primary promise: automated improvement against goals and metrics the customer sets

The product should not be positioned as only a workflow builder. Workflow is an implementation concept. The buyer cares more about outcomes:

- cleaner leads
- faster support
- safer releases
- lower cloud costs
- consistent content output
- better answer-engine visibility
- repeatable company operations

## Homepage Copy Direction

Recommended H1:

```text
AI automations that improve business outcomes.
```

Recommended core description:

```text
Runloop is an open-source AI automation platform. Set the goals and metrics. AI agents run the business process, measure the result, then improve the automation using your tools, knowledgebase, skills, evals, and approval rules.
```

The homepage should repeatedly connect the product loop to business outcomes:

- automation runs on real business work
- tools and MCPs provide context/action
- knowledgebase and skills preserve what works
- evals and metrics measure quality
- AI agents propose, keep, revert, or promote improvements based on goal movement
- repeated runs improve the automation instead of only producing another output

## Copy Rules

- Prefer `automation` over `workflow` in headings and CTAs.
- Use `workflow` only when describing internal mechanics or builder structure.
- Prefer business nouns in headings: support, QA, revenue, cloud costs, content, operations.
- Tie technical primitives to value: skills, MCPs, knowledgebase, evals, metrics, models.
- On the How page, explain the loop in this order: goals and metrics -> automation steps -> agents and MCP tools -> evaluation -> automated improvement -> skills/knowledgebase.
- Avoid sounding like a generic no-code automation tool; the differentiator is the improvement loop.
- Avoid sounding like only a developer framework; the buyer should understand the business outcome before the architecture.
